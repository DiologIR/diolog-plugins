Key findings
highlights

The Q4 FY26 quarterly update is a substantive operational and strategic disclosure containing numerous financial metrics, forward-looking statements and governance updates. The three indexed comparison documents are narrow procedural and administrative announcements.

None of them contains operational, financial-performance or guidance content against which the bulk of the new document's material claims can be tested. Consequently the majority of claims resolve to insufficient evidence, and no prior quarterly reports, the 11 June 2026 Investor Day materials, or the FY26 and H1 results have been indexed. One point warrants human attention: the new document introduces Peter White as CFO delivering the CFO commentary, whereas the most recent indexed disclosure describes his appointment as an Executive Director. The interaction of the CFO title with the Executive Director role should be confirmed. Confidence is reduced by the limited and non-overlapping comparison set and the absence of the Investor Day source document that anchors most forward-looking claims.

  1. Medium The Q4 FY26 update quotes Peter White as CFO, but the most recent indexed disclosure describes his appointment as an Executive Director. When was the CFO role formalised and disclosed, and does he hold both positions?

Proposed answer

Mr Peter White was appointed an Executive Director effective 5 May 2026, as disclosed in the addendum of 5 June 2026, which set out his $450,000 base salary, performance bonus of up to 40%, and 3,000,000 options at a $0.60 exercise price. In the Q4 FY26 update he provides commentary in his capacity as Chief Financial Officer. The two roles are held concurrently. Future announcements will reference his full title consistently.

HighForward The FCF target, the dividend milestones and the leverage target are all attributed to the 11 June 2026 Investor Day. What assumptions and reasonable grounds support them?

Proposed answer

These targets were set out at the 11 June 2026 Investor Day, which details the assumptions behind the free cash flow improvement plan, the capital allocation framework and the dividend reinstatement pathway. The Q4 FY26 update reports supporting progress: an FCF-positive quarter, growth capex funded entirely from operating cash flow, net debt reduced to $36.6m, and completion of Phase 1 debt refinancing and workshop consolidation. We refer investors to the Investor Day materials for the full basis of the targets.

HighForward Every dividend milestone is marked completed or on track just five weeks after the Investor Day. What supports the on-track status for the later ones, such as Phase 2 debt restructure and net debt below 2x EBITDA?

Proposed answer

The completed milestones, being the cash buffer, the Phase 1 debt facility restructure with loan documents executed, and the cost savings and workshop resizing plan, are supported by actions executed during the quarter. For the forward milestones, the update reports an FCF-positive Q4 FY26, net debt reduced to $36.6m from $38.9m, and workshop centralisation complete. Phase 2 restructuring is progressing in line with plan. These milestones remain subject to execution and prevailing conditions, and we will update the market as each is achieved.

MediumGeneral Engineering contracted work in hand has fallen from $15.4m to $14.6m to $11.6m, with no material contract awards in the quarter. What is the outlook for the division?

Proposed answer

As disclosed, Engineering market conditions remain subdued and contracted work in hand declined during the quarter. The division continues to pursue government infrastructure and industrial opportunities with a developing tender pipeline, supported in the interim by smaller fabrication and onsite maintenance works, and by the St Mary's Train Station upgrade nearing completion. Near-term momentum is led by the Mining equipment hire division, where daily hire rates rose 0.9% on the prior quarter and 7% on Q2 FY26.

MediumGeneral The update flags expected non-cash impairments of $1.5m to $2.0m and $0.4m of restructuring costs. Are these captured in the reported net debt and free cash flow, and could the impairment exceed the range?

Proposed answer

The impairment is a non-cash item arising from the balance sheet review and does not affect net debt or free cash flow. The $0.4m restructuring cost was incurred in the quarter in connection with the workshop and support services restructuring. The figures in this update are unaudited. The range reflects management's current assessment pending completion of the balance sheet review, and the final amount will be confirmed in the audited accounts.

LowGovernance Following the inadvertent Listing Rule 10.14 breach disclosed on 22 May 2026, what further governance measures has the Board implemented, and is the Robinson appointment part of that response?

Proposed answer

The Board previously confirmed the breach was an isolated, inadvertent incident and remediated it by arranging for the affected shares to be sold on-market with the proceeds donated to charity. The appointment of Glen Robinson as an Independent Non-Executive Director and Audit & Risk Committee member further strengthens the Board's governance and oversight capability, bringing over 40 years of mining and resources experience.
