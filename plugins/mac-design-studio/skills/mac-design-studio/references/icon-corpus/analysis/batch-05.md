### final-cut-pro-2025-11-10 — Final Cut Pro (2025-11-10)

notable: Apple's own — proves a full-rainbow fill works when structure (the clapper) supplies contrast and identity; the 2025 pass softens edges toward glass

## Batch summary

**Era distribution (n=50):** big-sur 22 · skeuomorphic 12 · liquid-glass 8 · flat-brand 6 · custom-web 2

**Dominant palette families:** Blue is overwhelmingly the default utility hue (helm, xcode, tap-forms, shellcut, task-flow, parachute, search-everything, ai-localization, idroid-adjacent — 10+ icons), followed by purple/violet (readkit, scrivener, tot, pockity, musicsmart, elytra, feeder) and warm orange/cream (marsedit, parcel, arkyve, quip). Greens cluster on cleaner/Android apps. The strongest icons run monochrome-plus-one-accent (flighty's zero-color, parcel's all-warm, helm's blue+frost); the weakest run tone-on-tone so far the glyph dissolves (search-everything, spin).

**Recurring glyph devices:** the checkmark (things, task-flow), the document/page stack (readkit, pockity, find-any-file, eaglefiler), the diagonal tool on blueprint-grid (xcode and its imitators tap-forms, ai-localization, helm's grid), the hardware-device face (macrowave, atem, dj-mix-pads, rhythm-pads, file-multi-tool), the gauge/dial (system-dashboard, magic-disk-benchmark, disk-diet), the magnifier (search-everything, find-any-file), and literal pun objects (parachute=backup, zipper=archive, sandwich=clipboard, scale=disk diet, spray bottle=cleaner).

**Material trends / Liquid Glass tells:** the 2025 grammar is clearly visible in the newest icons — near-white or pale tonal grounds (mindnode, elytra, parcel, bezel, system-dashboard), frosted translucent glyphs or panels (helm's wheel, musicsmart's note card, elytra's underwings), layered translucent shapes that multiply color where they overlap (mindnode, bezel), soft gel/clay extrusion replacing hard gloss (parcel, rhythm-pads vs. the older shellcut/alarm-clock gloss sweeps), and rim highlights standing in for outlines. Emissive accents (LEDs, dot-matrix, pad glow) are the accepted way to add a second hue on dark grounds. Hard diagonal gloss sweeps, photo textures (wood, wallpaper) and baked raster imagery now read as instant era-markers for "old."

**A-grade standouts:**
- **elytra** — the most complete Liquid Glass demonstration: rigid gel wing cases, genuinely frosted layered underwings, emissive amber core, pale neutral ground; metaphor and material tell one story.
- **parcel** — soft-clay single object on a tonal cream ground with an all-warm monochrome palette; the restraint formula an icon generator should default to.
- **flighty** — proves discipline beats saturation: pure black/white technical drawing with x-ray translucency; every line survives at 16px.
- **mindnode** — canonical layered-translucency-on-near-white; three hues, no outlines, overlap blending as the craft moment.
- **things / quip** — physical-metaphor storytelling (dimensional inbox tray; clipboard-as-sandwich) where wit and depth cues carry identity without palette noise.

**Most common failure modes:** (1) baked photoreal raster or photo texture pasted into a shaded icon (eaglefiler's eagle, alarm-clock's wood, monitorcontrol's wallpaper); (2) micro-detail and baked text that cannot survive icon scale (atem-exporter's 30 buttons, file-multi-tool's LCD date, shinytimer's numerals); (3) tone-on-tone glyphs with no silhouette contrast (search-everything, spin); (4) template genericism — the stock checkmark/globe/gradient with no ownable identity (task-flow, ai-app-localization); (5) era-drag — 2000s gloss sweeps and Aqua glass surviving unreconstructed (morph-age, shellcut's sheen).
