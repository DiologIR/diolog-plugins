## Batch summary

**Era distribution (n=50):** big-sur 22 · liquid-glass 11 · skeuomorphic 11 · flat-brand 5 · custom-web 1.
The liquid-glass count is driven almost entirely by Apple's 2026-02-04 refresh set (Compressor, Final Cut, Keynote, Logic, MainStage, Motion, Numbers, Pages, Pixelmator Pro, Configurator) plus one third party (Dropzone 5). Indies remain overwhelmingly Big Sur gel; a photoreal-skeuomorphic revival (Octavo, BRAW Toolbox, Plugin Station, Fast Collections, Gyroflow) is the strongest counter-current.

**Dominant palette families:** blue is by far the most common ground (9+ icons: #1E7BE8–#3E96F0 band), then violet/purple (6), warm amber/orange (6), green (3), crimson/red (3), and dark-neutral/carbon grounds (8). The strongest palettes in the batch are monochrome-plus-one-accent: Octavo's red book, Plugin Station's blue band, iA Writer's blue caret, Logic's pure silver. Apple's 26 set is strictly tone-on-tone — one hue family per icon, glyph differentiated by luminance/opacity only.

**Recurring glyph devices:** pen/pencil/writing tool (5: Lightweight Text Editor, Tap Forms, Pages, Bit Slicer, Pricetag), checkmark (TaskTab, OmniFocus), chart/gantt bars (Numbers, OmniPlan, OmniOutliner, Pastel), circular dial/ring/disc (iStat, Logic ×2, Nitro, [untitled], iFinance), layered stacks (Shortcut Bar folders, Configurator devices, Pixelmator diamonds), full-bleed physical object with no framed ground (Octavo, Hand Mirror, Plugin Station, BRAW), and 3D mascots (Owly, CleanMaster).

**Material trends / Liquid Glass tells:** the 2026 Apple formula is consistent and learnable — (1) deeper, more saturated vertical-gradient ground than Big Sur; (2) the legacy glyph rebuilt as frosted or tinted translucent gel/glass in the same hue family as the ground; (3) form defined by rim highlights and internal luminance, not outlines or drop shadows; (4) layered translucency for depth (Configurator is the purest case); (5) soft emissive bloom around the glyph. The indie mirror of this is the light-ground formula: near-white squircle + one saturated gel element (iA Writer, NotePlan, TextSniper, CleanMyMac). Gel extrusion with candy gloss remains the default indie material; true frosted translucency is still rare outside Apple.

**A-grade standouts:**
- **Plugin Station** — flawless metaphor (plug→plugins), full-bleed photoreal object, one accent hue; proof that skeuomorphism plus restraint beats gradient templates.
- **Apple Configurator** — the cleanest layered-translucency exemplar: depth built entirely from stacked frosted opacity and one rim light.
- **Keynote 26** — the iWork refresh distilled: legacy silhouette re-materialised in back-lit frosted glass on a deepened ground; the reference recipe for the era.
- **iA Writer** — owns a UI primitive (the caret) as the entire mark on a near-white ground; scales perfectly and anticipated the light Liquid Glass look years early.
- **Octavo** — museum-grade skeuomorphic render whose single red accent does all the work; warm neutral ground is rare and ownable.
- (Also cited: Dropzone 5 for third-party liquid glass with an emissive core; Logic Pro's brushed-metal platter as the canonical one-material icon; Alpenglow for colour-as-product.)

**Most common failure modes:** (1) template convergence — saturated gradient ground + flat white glyph with no ownable character (iFinance, TaskTab); (2) tone-on-tone silhouette loss — glyph hue too close to ground (Lightweight Text Editor's blue-on-blue, Motion's red-on-crimson risk); (3) detail that dies at small sizes — photoreal texture, tiny screens, leaf/star speckle (BRAW, Gyroflow, MacFamilyTree, [untitled]'s iridescence); (4) non-native execution — wrong silhouette and flat web outline style (Upscayl); (5) busy grounds fighting the glyph (Keyhole's warped grid); (6) App-Store-thumbnail garnish — sparkles and candy gloss standing in for an idea (Pricetag).
