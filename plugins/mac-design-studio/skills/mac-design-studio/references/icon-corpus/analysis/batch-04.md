## Batch summary

**Era distribution (n=50):** liquid-glass 43, big-sur 3 (Image Capture, iMovie, Claquette), skeuomorphic 2 (Chess, Black Ink 2), flat-brand 2 (Agenda, Craft), custom-web 0. This batch is dominated by Apple's macOS 26 Tahoe system set captured Nov 2025, so it is close to a ground-truth reference for the Liquid Glass language; the handful of off-era entries are legacy Apple assets not yet re-cut (Chess, iMovie, Image Capture) plus third parties.

**Dominant palette families:** (1) System blue gradients (#3E9CF8 → #1E6CE8) — App Store, Mail, Keynote, Weather, Bluetooth, Finder; (2) System green (#4CD45E → #30BE48) — Messages, Phone, FaceTime, Find My; (3) Red/coral (#F53E4E → #D42A2E) — Music, News, Dictionary, Photo Booth, Games; (4) Amber/gold (#FFB92E → #F08018) — Tips, Home, Notes, Agenda; (5) Neutral silver/greyscale for utilities (Disk Utility, VoiceOver, Font Book, Calculator); (6) Near-black charcoal (#1A1A1D) dark grounds for pro/system tools (Passwords, Icon Composer, Console, Activity Monitor, Magnifier, ColorSync, Audio MIDI). Accent discipline is strict: one hue family per icon, with multi-hue reserved for color-domain tools (ColorSync, Digital Color Meter, Game Center, Maps) and even then quarantined inside a shape or kept pastel.

**Recurring glyph devices:** white gel object on saturated single-hue gradient (the workhorse formula: Messages/Phone/FaceTime/Mail/Music/Keynote/Books/App Store); the-surface-is-the-glyph full-bleed artifacts (Clock, Notes, Calendar, Maps, Claquette); wrench-cradling-a-primitive for utilities (Disk Utility, ColorSync); emissive trace/text on dark (Activity Monitor, Console, Image Playground); layer stacks and nested extrusions (Icon Composer, Home); circular disc instruments (Time Machine, Find My); letterform-as-glyph (Dictionary, Font Book, News, Craft, Agenda).

**Material trends / Liquid Glass tells:** the era's fingerprint is everywhere in this batch — (a) frosted translucent glyphs where the ground color visibly bleeds through or blurs beneath (App Store's overlapping sticks, Finder's frosted half-face, Contacts' frosted card over colored tabs); (b) soft gel extrusion with top rim highlights and colored bounce light from the ground into white objects (Games' pink-tinged rocket, Messages/Phone mint undersides); (c) layered translucency with correct depth ordering and additive color blends at overlaps (Game Center bubbles, Freeform's glass square over the orange circle, Passwords' fanned keys); (d) emissive objects lighting their own same-hue ground (Tips bulb, Home's glowing nested rings); (e) two ground families only — saturated single-hue gradient, or near-white/near-black neutral with a colored gel object and soft cast shadow. Flatness is tolerated (Calendar, Notes, Clock) only when the artifact silhouette is iconic.

**A-grade standouts:** App Store (the canonical layered-frosted-translucency demo — three overlapping glass sticks with readable depth); Finder (opaque color vs frosted glass split in a single face — the era's thesis statement); Contacts (frosted pane physically over colored tabs, the most convincing glass-over-color read); Game Center (bubble overlaps producing genuine additive color mixing); Passwords and Icon Composer (dark-ground glass done with discipline — tinted translucent objects, rim lights, controlled triads); Activity Monitor and Tips (the two poles of emissive material: neon-on-dark and glow-on-same-hue); Calendar (proof the system also rewards radical flat abstraction — dates become dots).

**Common failure modes:** legacy 3D assets pasted into the new frame (Chess's photoreal wooden knight, iMovie's hard metallic bevel) whose specular language reads a generation old; baked raster photography inside an icon (Image Capture) with uncontrolled palette and poor small-size read; hard lacquer speculars vs the era's soft gel (Black Ink 2); vacant centers in full-bleed objects (Claquette's empty gradient screen); and for generators the trap this batch warns against most — rendering the white-object-on-hue formula without the translucency cues (ground bounce, rim light, frosted underlay) that separate Liquid Glass from a flat Big Sur re-tread.
