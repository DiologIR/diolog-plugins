## Batch summary

**Era distribution:** big-sur 35 · skeuomorphic 6 (desktop-curtain, flashcard-hero-lite, qreate, braw-toolbox, hype-4, unfolder) · custom-web 6 (party-mixer, angry-birds, cut-the-rope, frogger, illustrated, pac-man — five of six are game IP) · flat-brand 3 (davinci-resolve, artstudio-pro, popclip) · liquid-glass 0. This 2023–early-2024 window is peak Big Sur orthodoxy: flat-gel squircles, single accessories overhanging the tile, object-as-icon.

**Dominant palette families:** blue is by far the most common ground (source-files, s3-files, teleprompter, widgetkit-simulator, popclip, pac-man, mercury-weather, multi — est #1f7ae0–#2a86f0 band); then warm amber/orange grounds (logoist, virtualbuddy, gyroflow-toolbox ~#ffb420); dark charcoal/navy grounds for dev/pro tools (prompt-3, codepoint, ai-transcription, ai-photo, artstudio, timemachinestatus); Apple system utilities live in pure neutral silver. Accent discipline is strong: the best icons hold one accent hue (or one emissive) against a mono ground; the weakest pile three-plus metaphor colors.

**Recurring glyph devices:** the stuffed folder (source-files, s3-files — same template), the white document/card on colored grid (creativit, doneit, daily, pockity), the overhanging photoreal accessory (compass ×2, eyedropper, camera lens, hard drive, disc, HTML5 badge — Apple's own utilities model this), single letter/monogram (klack, font-file-browser, artstudio, prompt-3), object-as-icon filling the tile (klack keycap, qreate terminal, pockity notebook, dvd drive), emissive-glyph-on-dark for dev/AI tools (prompt-3, codepoint, ai-transcription, ai-photo, party-mixer), and page-curl reveals (daily, multi, font-file-browser).

**Material trends / Liquid Glass tells:** the corpus is mostly matte gel + one specular event, but two icons clearly anticipate the Liquid Glass era: **multi** (frosted translucent sheet blurring the ground, curl edge-light, prismatic refraction under the cursor) and **mercury-weather** (frosted backlit-acrylic cloud absorbing ground color, all-soft edges, luminous near-pastel gradient). Other proto-tells: rim-glow outlines (gametrack), bloom-heavy gradients (virtualbuddy), and gel extrusions with soft floor shadows on dark (codepoint). No near-white layered-translucency grounds yet — dark emissive and mid-chroma gel still dominate.

**A-grade standouts (6):**
- **multi** — layered frosted translucency with real refraction physics; the strongest pre-2025 preview of Liquid Glass material language.
- **unfolder** — the icon performs the app's function (3D shapes in, papercraft nets out); best concept-to-image storytelling in the batch.
- **klack** — one keycap, one letter, one material; perfect economy and scale-robustness.
- **gyroflow-toolbox** — flagship-grade object-on-field render whose telemetry screen states the app's exact job.
- **timemachinestatus** — wave-split two-tone clock; system-literate homage with an original signature and immaculate silhouette.
- **mercury-weather** — frosted-gel cloud as a genuine material study, two years ahead of the frosted-glyph trend.

**Common failure modes:** metaphor pile-ups that never resolve (hype-4's wood+lens+rulers+HTML5 badge; folder-colorizer close behind), template interchangeability (creativit, doneit — card-on-colored-grid with nothing ownable; s3-files/source-files sharing one body), content-free minimalism (popclip's bare split, aspect-ratio-x's floating pill in dead space), tone-on-tone silhouette collapse (illustrated's navy-on-navy puzzle; ios-app-installer's gray-on-white arrow), third-party logos baked into the tile (hype-4), and game IP dropped in with zero platform adaptation (all five arcade titles — gorgeous renders, no macOS vocabulary).
