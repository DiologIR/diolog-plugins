## Batch summary

**Era distribution (n=50):** big-sur 25 · skeuomorphic 13 · liquid-glass 5 · flat-brand 4 · custom-web 3

**Dominant palette families:** (1) single-hue saturated ground + white/neutral glyph (blues and purples lead: ~11 blue-ground, ~7 purple-ground icons); (2) warm amber/orange grounds (logoist, quick-notes, planny, screenfloat, scapple rim); (3) hot pink/magenta gradients (darkroom, vectorize, goodlinks, photosweeper); (4) a distinct near-white/silver-ground cluster (writestudio, prompt-3, retcon, googly-eyes, past-for-ichat, istatistica-pro) that is the strongest current-era signal. Multicolor appears almost only as *indexed* color — uniform chips/blades/cards carrying category meaning (soulver, fantastical, cardhop, photomill).

**Recurring glyph devices:** pens/pencils/markers (7: writestudio, logoist, openmark, coppice, coteditor, retcon, linea-link), folders/documents/cards (8), drives/hardware skeuomorphs (5: network-radar, notion-toolbox, disk-aid, disk-map, klack), gauges (2), literal-name puns (betterzip's bound ball, photomill's mill, extractor's zipper, coppice's coppiced branch), frame-as-border structures (stitcher, screenfloat, scapple, retcon, prompt-3), and before/after split panels (photo-refinery).

**Material trends:** the corpus splits into three material dialects. (a) *Soft gel/clay extrusion* is the majority dialect — rounded bevels, smooth top-light gradients, micro drop shadows (secret-folder, openmark, soulver, photomill, folders). (b) *Legacy photoreal skeuomorphism* persists in utility apps — brushed metal, chrome, fabric, wood (disk-aid, extractor, download-shuttle, coppice, winmail). (c) The emerging *Liquid Glass dialect*: frosted translucent glyphs that blur the ground through themselves (darkroom, apple-developer's tools), true refractive glass (corridorkey, text-catalyst), rim highlights replacing outlines, near-white/pastel-frost grounds (writestudio, prompt-3, retcon, istatistica pair), and layered translucency in grounds (darkroom's ribbons, photosweeper's frosted photo panes). Emissive/neon accents on dark grounds form a minor fourth dialect (ghostty, keyframe-toolbox, cotypist's cursor).

**A-grade standouts:**
- **darkroom** — the definitive Liquid Glass execution: frosted glyph sampling a layered-translucency ground, crisp silhouette, fearless saturation.
- **betterzip-6** — best conceptual metaphor in the batch (archive = bound moss bundle) rendered as a photoreal diorama without losing squircle discipline.
- **corridorkey** — most ambitious optics: refraction, inversion, and chromatic dispersion inside a single glass sphere; material realism as the entire idea.
- **googly-eyes / klack** — twin masters of restraint: one object, two or three tones, silhouette-proof at any size; both anticipate the near-white minimal era.
- **fantastical / soulver-3** — the disciplined-multicolor pattern: candy accents locked into uniform geometry so color reads as data, not decoration.

**Common failure modes:** (1) template genericism — saturated gradient + stock gel glyph with no ownable idea (secret-folder, quick-notes, folders); (2) additive composition — name-glyph bolted onto a generic base instead of fused (amethyst, disk-map); (3) legacy baggage — busy grounds and competing bevels carried from pre-Big-Sur marks (bbedit), non-squircle object silhouettes that now read dated (scriptstar, disk-aid); (4) text baked into the icon ("CSV" in notion-toolbox, letterforms as crutch in bbedit); (5) weak pair differentiation between sibling SKUs (istatistica vs pro); (6) over-literal realism drifting off-tone (winmail's knife); (7) density collapse at small sizes from three-plus stacked focal systems (network-radar, coppice, keyframe-toolbox).
