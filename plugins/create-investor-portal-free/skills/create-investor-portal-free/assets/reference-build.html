Mining and engineering services in the Hunter Valley region.

Alfabs Australia Limited fabricates structural steel, builds and hires underground mining equipment, coats it and transports it. Family founded in the 1950s, listed in June 2024, over 300 people across five sites in NSW and Queensland.

§ 06

Five questions, answered plainly

What new shareholders ask most. Each answer names the disclosure it comes from.

01 How do I buy AAL on the ASX?

Through any broker with ASX access, under the code AAL.

Alfabs Australia Limited trades on the Australian Securities Exchange as AAL. There is no secondary listing on any other exchange. A CHESS-sponsored broker holds the shares under your own Holder Identification Number, which keeps the holding in your name on the register. A custodial broker holds them on your behalf, which is usually cheaper and one step further removed. Alfabs does not endorse any broker.

02 How is Alfabs capitalised?

Equity plus bank debt, with the founding family holding the majority.

The group listed in June 2024 following a restructure and remains founder led, with the family holding a majority of the shares on issue. Phase 1 of the debt refinancing completed during Q4 FY26. The capital structure as at 30 June 2026:

03 When does the dividend come back?

No date has been given. A net-debt milestone has.

Alfabs paid a maiden dividend in February 2025 and subsequently suspended distributions while the group refinanced and restructured. At the Investor Day held on 11 June 2026, the Board set out the condition for reconsidering distributions: net debt below 2x EBITDA, targeted for the second half of FY27. That presentation was released to the market on 18 June 2026. Net debt has moved from A$38.9M at 31 March 2026 to A$36.6M at 30 June 2026.

04 I already hold AAL. Where do I change my details?

At the share registry, not here. Which route depends on how you hold.

Alfabs does not hold or maintain the register. Computershare Investor Services does. What you can do depends on whether your holding is broker sponsored or issuer sponsored, and the identifier on your statement tells you which:

HIN

Starts with X. CHESS sponsored, held through your broker.

Address and bank detail changes go through your broker, not the registry.

SRN

Starts with I. Issuer sponsored, held directly on the register.

Changes go to Computershare Investor Centre, using your SRN and registered postcode.

The registry handles address changes, bank and payment instructions, annual report elections, tax file number or ABN notification, communication preferences, and lost holding statements. Your HIN or SRN is printed on any holding statement or dividend advice.

Overseas holders verify with their country rather than a postcode. Alfabs cannot look up or change a holding on your behalf.

Investor relations, through the form in the next section.
